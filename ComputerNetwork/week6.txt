 Digital logic
 Dividing circuit consisting of XOR
gates and a shift register
 Shift register is a string of 1-bit
storage devices
 Each device has an output line,
which indicates the value currently
stored, and an input line
 At discrete time instants, known
as clock times, the value in the
storage device is replaced by the
value indicated by its input line
 The entire register is clocked
simultaneously, causing a 1-bit
shift along the entire register
